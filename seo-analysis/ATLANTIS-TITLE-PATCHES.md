## How to apply

For each entry below, update three things in the per-route HTML / metadata layer:

1. The `<title>` tag.
2. `<meta name="description" content="...">` (kept ≤155 chars to avoid SERP truncation).
3. `<meta property="og:title" content="...">` (mirror the new title for share previews).
4. Also mirror to `<meta name="twitter:title">` and `<meta property="og:description">` if the route emits them.

These titles target user intent (cost / pass rate / year / decision criteria) rather than generic descriptors. The CTR rationale is in `seo-analysis/SEO-AUDIT-2026-05-15.md` — these 10 pages alone account for ~36k impressions / 90 days at an average CTR of 0.5%, against a position-7 expected benchmark of 3-5%.
