NDT Methods

Inspection Services

All major NDT methods available through our certified provider network.

UT

Ultrasonic Testing

High-frequency sound waves to detect internal flaws, measure thickness, and characterize materials.

Learn more RT

Radiographic Testing

X-ray and gamma ray imaging for internal defect detection in welds, castings, and assemblies.

Learn more MT

Magnetic Particle Testing

Surface and near-surface flaw detection in ferromagnetic materials using magnetic fields.

Learn more PT

Penetrant Testing

Surface-breaking defect detection using fluorescent or visible dye penetrant methods.

Learn more PAUT

Phased Array UT

Advanced ultrasonic imaging with electronic beam steering for detailed weld and component inspection.

Learn more ET

Eddy Current Testing

Electromagnetic induction for surface crack detection and conductivity measurement in metals.
