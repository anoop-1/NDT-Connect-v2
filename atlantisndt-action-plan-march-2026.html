The site has 542 routes in App.tsx but the prerender script only covers ~85 core routes + method×city combos. Hundreds of city-specific pages (eddy-current-testing-houston, penetrant-testing-dubai, etc.) serve an empty JS shell to Googlebot. Google sees no content → Discovered not indexed / Soft 404.

Impact: This is the #1 reason for 222 not-indexed pages and low organic visibility.

The submitted sitemap has 711 URLs but GSC reports 0 indexed from it. Either the sitemap wasn't properly submitted, or Google can't process the content behind the URLs (SPA rendering issue). The sitemap itself is well-formed XML.

HIGH Client-Side Rendering (SPA) Hurting SEO

SEOHead component uses useEffect() to inject meta tags at runtime. Googlebot's JS rendering is delayed and unreliable. The prerender script injects meta tags into static HTML for covered routes, but uncovered routes serve the default meta tags from index.html. This means Google may see generic titles/descriptions for 400+ pages.

8. Traffic Cycle Analysis & Fix

Why Traffic is Cyclic (10 clicks → 3 clicks pattern)

This is normal B2B weekday/weekend behavior. NDT is an industrial B2B sector — engineers and procurement managers search during work hours (Mon-Fri), not weekends. The pattern will always exist, but the amplitude matters more than the cycle.

How to Reduce the Amplitude

  • Target B2C-adjacent keywords: "NDT career guide", "NDT salary", "what is NDT testing" — these get weekend searches from job seekers and students
  • Target international audiences: Weekend in US = weekday in Middle East. Dubai/India training pages will get traffic on US weekends
  • Build YouTube presence: Video content gets searched on weekends; embed NDT method demonstrations
  • Create evergreen tools: NDT method selector, certification cost calculator — interactive tools get weekend traffic from career planners
