Cyclic Traffic Pattern — Diagnosis

The traffic fluctuates between ~2-10 clicks/day in a repeating cycle. This is normal for B2B/industrial content — NDT professionals search during weekdays (Mon-Fri), and traffic drops 50-70% on weekends. The dips consistently align with Saturdays/Sundays.

How to flatten the cycle: The weekday/weekend dip cannot be fully eliminated since your audience is industrial professionals. However, you can reduce its impact by: (1) Adding consumer-education content ("what is NDT testing", "NDT career guide") that attracts students/job-seekers who search on weekends, (2) Targeting international markets across timezones, (3) Building email/social traffic sources that don't depend on search intent timing.

The Math

To reach 1,000 clicks/day you need approximately:

Option A (CTR focus): 10,000 impressions/day × 10% CTR = 1,000 clicks
Option B (Volume focus): 25,000 impressions/day × 4% CTR = 1,000 clicks
Option C (Balanced): 15,000 impressions/day × 6.7% CTR = 1,000 clicks

Realistic path: Option C — improve both impressions (from 355 → 15,000 = 42x) and CTR (from 1% → 6.7% = 6.7x). This requires: getting 450+ pages indexed, improving average position to top 5, and optimizing all title tags/meta descriptions.

Honest assessment: 1,000 clicks/day is achievable but will take 4-6 months of sustained effort. The site is currently at 0.36% of target. Immediate wins (fixing indexing, CTR optimization) can get to 50-100 clicks/day within 4-6 weeks.
